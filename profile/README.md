### [Pulse](https://github.com/Vaylo-Studios/pulse) - AI Operator OS

**The AI operator platform Vaylo runs on - live and self-serve at [pulse.vaylostudios.com](https://pulse.vaylostudios.com).**

Your business gets a brain: an AI receptionist on your phone line, autonomous lead follow-up, a morning briefing, and a NeuralOS canvas visualizing every agent in real time. Run it yourself on your own machine, or have us deploy and configure it in a two-week sprint with Build My Pulse.

**Architecture**
- **Pulse Hub** - shared state, agent registry, run history, telemetry
- **NeuralOS Overlay** - live canvas visualizing every agent and strand in real time
- **Memory Cortex** - SOPs, context, institutional memory - executable in one click
- **Strand Execution** - high-leverage workflows captured as reusable one-click automations
- **Mobile Companion** - monitor and trigger from anywhere
- **Daily AI Digest** - your business, summarized every morning
